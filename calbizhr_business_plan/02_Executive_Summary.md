## Executive Summary

Calbizhr (operating as **BizHR**) is a California-focused Human Resources compliance consultancy built specifically to serve small-to-medium businesses (5–75 employees) across high-stakes, litigious industries in the San Gabriel Valley and Greater Los Angeles area.

### The Problem
California maintains arguably the most complex and punitive labor law environment in the United States. Recent legislative mandates—including **SB 553** (Workplace Violence Prevention), **AB 1825 / SB 1343** (Mandatory Harassment Prevention Training), **SB 294** (Know Your Rights Act), **SB 642** (Pay Transparency Expansion), and **PAGA** (Labor Code Private Attorneys General Act) reform—place an immense legal compliance burden on small business owners. Most small businesses operate without in-house HR departments and cannot afford traditional employment law firm rates ($300–$500+/hour) or heavy national PEO retainers ($1,000–$5,000+/month), leaving them acutely vulnerable to catastrophic litigation and regulatory penalties.

### The Solution & Service Model
BizHR closes this gap by delivering **public-sector-grade HR compliance infrastructure** tailored for small businesses at flat, transparent, and affordable price points. Our three-tier delivery model consists of:
1. **Free / Low-Barrier Entry:** AI-powered self-assessment tools (*Strategic Audit Engine* & *Policy Architect*) and $75 credited initial consultations.
2. **Modular Project Deliverables:** Fixed-fee compliance packages ($349–$2,800) covering Workplace Violence Prevention Plans (WVPP), Employee Handbooks, Harassment Prevention Training, and IIPP Safety Programs.
3. **Ongoing Fractional HR Retainers:** Dedicated recurring compliance partnership ($650/month average) providing ongoing policy updates, investigation guidance, and risk management.

### Founder Advantage
BizHR is founded and led by **Mario Espindola, MPA, IPMA-SCP**, who brings over 10 years of municipal HR leadership experience overseeing $60M+ HR budgets and 1,500+ employee workforces across California municipalities (Santa Ana, Anaheim, Fountain Valley). This background combines high-level public administrative rigor with a deep personal mission to protect independent local business owners.

### Target Market & Verticals
While serving general shift-based small businesses (restaurants, retail, medical/dental clinics, auto, logistics), BizHR specifically targets three high-stakes priority verticals:
- **Cannabis (Dispensaries & Cultivation):** Maturing past informal startup operations into regulated corporate HR.
- **Construction & Skilled Trades:** High OSHA scrutiny, worker classification challenges, and safety mandates.
- **Hospitality & Nightlife (Bars, Restaurants, Hotels):** SB 553 workplace violence compliance and high-turnover harassment training requirements.

### Financial Outlook & Ramp
Beginning with a single beta client while maintaining lean overhead, BizHR is projected to scale to 45 active clients (25 recurring retainers) by Year 3 (2029). Total revenue is projected to grow from **$19,140** in Year 1 to **$108,600** in Year 2 (crossing break-even), reaching **$255,000** in Year 3 with a net income of **$163,000** (76% recurring retainer revenue).
